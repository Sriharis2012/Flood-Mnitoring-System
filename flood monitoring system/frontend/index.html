How Flood Sense 360 works

An ESP32 reads the water level sensor, lights red, yellow and green LEDs, sounds a buzzer and streams live data to this dashboard. Two ideas from mathematics turn raw readings into warnings.

Differential equation

The water level changes continuously. Its rate of rise tells us where the level is heading and when it will reach the flood line.

dh/dt = Q_in ÷ A h(t) = h₀ + r·t

Integration

The rate of rise adds up over time to give the total water gained and the flood exposure.

ΔV = ∫ Q dt E = ∫ L dt

Live sensing

Green is safe, yellow with a beep means rising, and blinking red with fast beeps means flood. The dashboard shows the same status in real time.
